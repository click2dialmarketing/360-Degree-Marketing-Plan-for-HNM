## 4) 90-Day Growth Architecture (Awareness → Demand → Lead → Visit → Close)

### Funnel Design
1. **Awareness:** Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Meta Video/Reels, local influencer collaboration.
2. **Demand Capture:** Search ads (high-intent keywords), GBP optimization, local SEO pages.
3. **Lead Conversion:** Dedicated landing pages, instant form ads, click-to-call, WhatsApp funnel.
4. **Nurture:** CRM follow-up cadence (0 min, 2 hrs, 24 hrs, 72 hrs).
5. **Sales Enablement:** Structured consult script + trial conversion offer + membership close journey.

### Core Offer Engine (for 90-day campaigns)
- **Offer A:** Complimentary “Posture & Core Assessment” (high-intent lead magnet).
- **Offer B:** Premium Intro Trial + Consultation (paid or controlled free with qualification).
- **Offer C:** Couple Transformation Program intro.
- **Offer D:** Corporate Pilates mobility session pilot.

## 5) Platform-wise Activities (What must be done)

## 5.1 Instagram (Organic + Paid)
**Organic:**
- Reels (educational + authority + transformation + trainer face content).
- Stories daily: polls, FAQs, slots, member wins, behind-the-scenes.
- Carousels: pain-point myth busting, form corrections, premium outcomes.
- UGC/Testimonial amplification with branded templates.

**Paid:**
- Reels Ads for awareness.
- Lead form + WhatsApp ads for consultation booking.
- Retargeting warm engagers with social proof and trial CTA.

**Cadence:**
- 5 Reels/week, 3 carousel posts/week, Stories daily (8–15 frames/day).

## 5.2 Meta (Facebook Ads + FB page)
- Full-funnel campaign stack (Awareness, Traffic, Lead Gen, Retargeting).
- Hyperlocal targeting (Gurugram + affluent clusters).
- Creative testing matrix (hooks, thumbnails, CTA variations).
- Click-to-call and WhatsApp objective testing.
- Weekly A/B testing and budget reallocation by CPL quality.

## 5.3 LinkedIn
- Founder/instructor authority content (thought leadership on movement, productivity, posture).
- Corporate wellness outreach campaign (HR/Admin decision makers).
- Lead Gen Forms for B2B studio partnerships (employee wellness packages).
- Monthly webinar/mini workshop for corporate audience.

## 5.4 Website + Landing Pages
- Build high-converting pillar pages:
  - /pilates-gurgaon
  - /reformer-pilates-gurgaon
  - /posture-correction-gurgaon
  - /premium-pilates-trial-gurgaon
- Dedicated campaign landing pages with:
  - Hero trust stack, before/after narratives, coach credentials.
  - CTA hierarchy: Call / WhatsApp / Book Trial.
  - FAQ objection handling + pricing anchors.
- Implement GA4, GTM, Meta Pixel, CAPI, conversion events, CRM source tagging.

## 5.5 Google Business Profile (GMB)
- Profile completeness and category optimization.
- Weekly posts (offers, transformation stories, festival/wellness themes).
- Review acquisition SOP (minimum 25 new reviews over 90 days).
- Review response SLA: <24 hours.
- High-quality photos/videos uploaded weekly.
- UTM-tagged GBP links to measure calls/directions/site visits.

## 5.6 YouTube
- 2 Shorts/week + 1 long-form authority video/week.
- YouTube Ads: Shorts + in-stream for premium awareness.
- Remarketing audiences from video viewers to lead campaigns.

## 5.7 Google Ads (Search + Performance Max Local Signals)
- High-intent search campaigns:
  - “Pilates near me”, “Reformer Pilates Gurgaon”, “Best Pilates studio Gurgaon”, etc.
- Call ads during operating hours.
- Location assets + local action tracking.
- Branded + competitor + category keyword buckets.
- Weekly negative keyword and query mining.

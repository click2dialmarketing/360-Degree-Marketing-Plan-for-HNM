**1. Tulip Melrose I ad set — CRITICAL ($164.79 CPC)**
- Spent $164.79 to generate a single click. This is the worst-performing ad set across both accounts.

- "tulip melrose rg - Gurugram Only" ($59.70) and "tulip melrose rg 2" ($59.56) both delivered impressions but zero link clicks.

**3. Tulip Melrose campaign family — 83% of spend, 44% of clicks**
- $430.72 (83% of Investza Homes budget) has gone to Tulip Melrose variants. Combined result: 4 clicks at an average CPC of $107.68.
- **Action:** Consolidate all Tulip Melrose ad sets into a single test campaign with a hard daily cap of ₹500/day until performance improves.

**4. 4S Aurrum — only bright spot, undersized budget**
- CTR of 1.33% and CPC of $17.62 are the best numbers in this account by a wide margin.
- It received only $88.09 (17% of budget) while underperforming campaigns consumed the rest.
- **Action:** Reallocate freed budget from paused Tulip Melrose ad sets into 4S Aurrum. Test duplicate ad sets with slightly varied audiences to scale safely.

- **Action:** Review audience targeting and creative. If this is a cold-audience awareness campaign, ensure the objective is set to Reach/Awareness rather than Traffic — a mismatched objective would explain the high CPM.

**5. P3 Duo Pilates — Consolidate**
